# 📊 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis & Prediction

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn in the telecom industry using SQL, Python, Machine Learning, and Power BI.

The objective is to identify customers who are likely to leave the company and provide actionable business recommendations to improve customer retention.

---

## Business Problem

Telecom companies lose customers every month.

Using customer demographics, services, billing information, and contracts, we predict churn and identify the major factors affecting customer retention.

---

## Dataset

Records: 7,043 Customers

## Key Insights

- Month-to-Month customers have the highest churn.

- Customers with Fiber Optic Internet churn more.

- High Monthly Charges increase churn probability.

- Customers with Tech Support are less likely to churn.

- Long-term contracts significantly reduce churn.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Offer yearly contract discounts.

- Improve customer support.

- Target high-risk customers with retention offers.

- Reward loyal customers.
